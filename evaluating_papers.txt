2) Attention is All You Need by Vaswani et al., 2017
This is arguably one of the most important papers of the last ten years. The proposed Attention mechanism is the basis of modern generative AI models, such as LLMs. They are very thorough in describing the architecture and algorithms and provide a link to their open source code. Amazing.
